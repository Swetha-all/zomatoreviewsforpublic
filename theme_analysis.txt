ZOMATO PRODUCT FEEDBACK ANALYSIS - THEME BREAKDOWN
June 2026

THEME: Customer Support
Reviews: 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 17, 18, 20, 25
Count: 9
Top Pain Point: Tickets get closed (by human or AI) without actual resolution, and photo/proof evidence is dismissed with no explanation
Suggested Fix: Auto-escalate to a human agent if an AI bot response is rejected twice; block ticket closure until the customer confirms resolution
Priority: High

THEME: Delivery & Logistics
Reviews: 2, 7, 9, 13, 15, 21
Count: 6
Top Pain Point: Food goes missing/left unattended by delivery agents, or arrives cold because orders are clubbed with others
Suggested Fix: Require photo/OTP proof-of-drop at doorstep; auto-detect and prevent clubbing when it risks exceeding delivery-time SLA
Priority: High

THEME: Payments & Refunds
Reviews: 4, 8, 11, 14, 23
Count: 5
Top Pain Point: Money deducted for cancelled/unconfirmed orders or promised refunds never credited
Suggested Fix: SLA-based auto-refund (e.g., auto-credit if unresolved in 48 hrs) + a real-time refund status tracker
Priority: High

THEME: Order Accuracy (wrong/missing items)
Reviews: 3, 5, 6, 25
Count: 4
Top Pain Point: Wrong dish or missing items delivered, with photo evidence ignored during complaints
Suggested Fix: Mandatory packer-side item photo checklist matched against order; auto-approve refunds when customer photo contradicts packing photo
Priority: High

THEME: UI/UX
Reviews: 12, 16, 22
Count: 3
Top Pain Point: Reordering past meals takes too many clicks despite otherwise clean design
Suggested Fix: Add a one-tap "Reorder" shortcut on the home screen/order history
Priority: Medium

THEME: Pricing
Reviews: 19
Count: 1
Top Pain Point: App prices run higher than in-store despite good restaurant variety
Suggested Fix: Show a transparent price breakdown (platform fee vs. restaurant markup)
Priority: Low
