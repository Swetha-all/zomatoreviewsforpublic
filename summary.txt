PRODUCT FEEDBACK ANALYSIS - ZOMATO
Executive Summary - June 2026

Analysis of 25 user reviews reveals four high-priority issue clusters that together
account for 24 of 25 reviews mentioning at least one problem.

Customer support is the single biggest driver of dissatisfaction (9 mentions). The
core failure isn't the presence of problems, but that the resolution loop breaks:
tickets close without confirmation, and photo evidence submitted by customers is
dismissed without explanation. This compounds every other issue on this list, since
a bad delivery or wrong order becomes a bad experience only when support fails to
fix it.

Delivery & logistics (6 mentions) and order accuracy (4 mentions) point to a shared
root cause: process breakdowns are happening upstream (at the pickup/packing and
drop stages) with no verification layer to catch them before the customer is
affected.

Payments & refunds (5 mentions) shows a pattern of the company being fast to charge
but slow to reverse - a trust issue that compounds support frustration since these
are often the same complaint threads.

RECOMMENDED PRIORITY ORDER FOR Q1:
1. Support resolution SLA + human escalation trigger
   (highest leverage - fixes downstream frustration across all other categories)
2. Photo/OTP proof-of-drop for delivery
3. Auto-refund SLA for cancelled/unconfirmed orders
4. Packer-side photo verification for order accuracy

UI/UX and pricing transparency are lower priority - they're friction points, not
trust-breakers, and can move to Q2.
